\chapter{Abstract}\label{Chap:Abstract}

The research topic addressed in this paper focuses on the design of a controller with double integral effect for unstable systems with sticking-slip phenomena. The results of this research were developed specifically in the context of the magnetic levitation system in its unstable configuration. The representation of the model was based on the parameters of the literature, which were obtained through analysis in laboratory experiments related to this device. Using digital simulations, a study was carried out on the nonlinear dynamics associated with Coulomb friction, the dead zone (which results from the combination of these two phenomena and is known as the sticking-sliding effect) and the electromagnetic force, plus the instability of the system due to the presence of multiple equilibrium points. The model was linearized in both its stable and unstable configurations, and a linear controller with double integral action was designed on the unstable model; the same controller also stabilizes the stable configuration. The controller was first evaluated on the linear model and on the nonlinear model without stick-slip, and then with the dead zone and static friction included. Finally, monitoring and regulation tests were carried out to validate the effectiveness of the designed controllers in stabilizing the system.
